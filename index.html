        Water lushy pubic mons
        And give them lots of sunlight
        Sprink' a little fert and dirt
        To help them exceed cow-height

        I'm not much a landscaper
        It's au-oh-so-naturale
        They grow taller than the cows
        I release from Mons corral

        Wander blindly through my brush
        In search of drinking water
        Though they only get a sip
        When I'm a pondly squatter

        Rushing water licks my leaves
        And quenches all my cattle
        Then to keep my undies dry
        Walk with a duckly waddle.

        - Milk, Mons Frère Gardenrère
